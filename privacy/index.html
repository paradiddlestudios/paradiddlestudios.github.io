Privacy Policy

Bajutsu Love · Effective 11 August 2026

Bajutsu Love does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

The game runs entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests while you play.

What is stored on your device

So that you can continue a game in progress, the app saves a single local file containing:

  • the player name you type at the start of the game
  • your story progress: current day, chapter, and scene state
  • gameplay values: ability experience, relationship levels, in-game money, and which one-time events and guidance cards you have seen
  • your settings: volume levels and text speed

This file is written to the app's own private storage area, provided by iOS or Android. It never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to any server we control. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you enter a real name as your player name, that name is stored only in that local file, and only so the characters can address you.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or transmit personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not have any.
  • We do not require an account, email address, or sign-in.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs that collect data.

Purchases

If the app is sold or offers purchases, those transactions are handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We never receive your payment details.

Children's privacy

The game deals with mature emotional themes and is rated accordingly on each store; a content notice appears before the story begins. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app collects no personal information at all.

Third-party services used to make the game

For transparency, and separate from anything that happens on your device: parts of the game's art, character voices, and music were produced with generative AI tools during development. Those tools were used offline in production. They are not contacted by the shipped app, and no player data is ever sent to them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect changes unless the app itself gains new capabilities.
