Privacy Policy

Fun Felt World: Monsters · Effective 29 October 2026

Fun Felt World: Monsters does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

The game is made for young children. It runs entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data, and it makes no network requests while your child plays. Nothing in the game asks for a name, an email address, a photo, or anything typed in, and it does not use the camera, microphone, or location.

What is stored on your device

So that your child's world is still there next time, the app saves two small files:

  • the play world: the monsters your child has made (their shapes, colours, outfits and faces), where each one is, and the homes they have built
  • which packs have been unlocked with an in-app purchase

These files are written to the app's own private storage area, provided by iOS. They never leave your device. We cannot see them, and they are not backed up to any server we control. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or transmit personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • We do not track anyone across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not have any.
  • We do not require an account, email address, or sign-in.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs that collect data.

Purchases

Extra places, outfits and friends can be unlocked with in-app purchases. Every purchase, and every link that leaves the game, is behind a parental gate: a question for a grown-up to answer before anything happens. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple under its own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details or your Apple ID.

Children's privacy

Fun Felt World: Monsters is designed for children aged about four to nine. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the app collects no personal information at all. That is how we meet the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Australian Privacy Principles and similar laws elsewhere: there is nothing to collect, store or disclose.

Third-party services used to make the game

For transparency, and separate from anything that happens on your device: parts of the game's art, sounds, and music were produced with generative AI tools during development. Those tools were used in production only. They are not contacted by the shipped app, and no player data is ever sent to them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect changes unless the app itself gains new capabilities.
