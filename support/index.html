Bajutsu Love

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL and a person will read it. Please include your device and, if something went wrong, what you were doing when it did.

Where is my save?

The game keeps one save, stored privately on your device and written automatically at the end of each in-game day. It is not synced to any server, so it does not travel between devices, and uninstalling the app removes it permanently.

Can I start over?

Yes. Choosing New Game from the title screen begins a fresh term. The game will warn you first if it would overwrite a game in progress.

I am stuck, or I missed something

You cannot lock yourself out of the story — the term always runs its full sixty days and always reaches an ending. Skill checks that do not go your way change how a scene plays out rather than stopping it. If a bond has gone quiet, spending time with that character is what warms it up again.

Content

The story deals with mature emotional themes, including family conflict, serious illness, and periods of real despair. A content notice appears before the story begins. The game is rated accordingly on each store.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds go through them rather than through us — via the App Store or Google Play purchase history on your account.

Something is broken

Please tell us. Include your device model, your operating system version, and roughly where in the term you were. Reports like that are the fastest route to a fix, and they are genuinely welcome.
